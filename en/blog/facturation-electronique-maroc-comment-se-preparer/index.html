E-invoicing in Morocco: what is confirmed and how to prepare

28 August 2026

E-invoicing is becoming a defining programme for finance, tax and technology teams. Beyond replacing paper, it turns the invoice into a controlled, traceable data flow that enterprise systems can process directly.

What official sources confirm

In its 2024 activity report, Morocco’s General Tax Directorate states that it launched the electronic invoicing system project. Functional and technical specifications were finalised and development started. The stated objectives include transaction transparency, tax compliance and stronger controls.

The detailed timetable, scope, technical formats and rollout rules remain subject to officially published legislation and specifications. A responsible readiness programme therefore separates no-regret work from decisions that depend on the final framework.

Five workstreams to start now

  • Map invoicing journeys, post-invoice events and business ownership.
  • Assess customer, tax, contractual and payment data quality.
  • Identify the ERP, TMS, accounting tools, portals and interfaces in scope.
  • Design a decoupled, secure and observable integration architecture.
  • Set up programme governance across Finance, Tax, IT, Security and Operations.

Build an adaptable architecture

A sound target isolates regulatory logic behind an integration layer able to convert, validate, transmit and trace flows. This limits changes to core systems and keeps formats, controls and channels adaptable as the official framework evolves.

Turn compliance into operational value

Better structured data reduces rekeying, supports reconciliation, accelerates exception handling and improves order-to-cash visibility. The programme can therefore improve operational quality, cash steering and partner experience as well as compliance.
